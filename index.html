QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

What exactly does TileIQ do?

TileIQ is a complete business app for tilers — measure a room, calculate materials and labour, send a professional quote, manage the job through to completion, invoice, and export to your accounting software. The AI Receptionist can also answer calls and take enquiries when you're on the tools.

Is it free to try?

Yes — TileIQ is free to start with a 30-day free trial, no credit card required.

Do I need to be VAT registered or run a limited company?

No — TileIQ works for sole traders and small businesses at any stage, VAT registered or not.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes — TileIQ is available on both the App Store and Google Play, and your data syncs across devices.

Can I use TileIQ without a Leica Disto?

Absolutely — a Leica Disto makes measuring faster, but TileIQ works just as well with measurements you enter manually.

Does TileIQ work without an internet connection?

Yes — you can measure, quote and manage jobs offline, and everything syncs automatically once you're back online.

Is my data safe?

Yes — your data is encrypted and backed up, and only accessible to you. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — there are no contracts. Cancel anytime from within the app or your account settings.

What if I need help getting started?

Just message us via the Contact page and we'll get back to you.
