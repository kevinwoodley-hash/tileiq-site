Why every tiler needs a simple job management system.

25 August 2026 · 5 min read

Running jobs from a notebook, a diary and a few text threads works right up until it doesn't — a double-booked Tuesday, a quote nobody chased, an enquiry that went cold because it got buried under three other conversations. None of it looks like a big problem on its own. All of it adds up.

What actually goes wrong without a system

The failures are rarely dramatic. They're small and quiet:

  • Enquiries go cold. An enquiry that isn't followed up within a day or two often just goes to the next tiler who replies.
  • Nothing gets chased. An unanswered quote or an unpaid invoice relies entirely on you remembering to follow it up — and eventually, one doesn't get remembered.
  • Jobs get double-booked or forgotten. Scheduling from memory or a paper diary makes clashes more likely, not less, especially once you're juggling more than a couple of jobs at once.
  • Customer history lives nowhere. When a past customer calls back, their previous job, quote and notes should be one lookup away — not a search through old messages.

One pipeline, not five places

A proper system doesn't need to be complicated. It needs every job to move through the same clear stages — enquiry, surveyed, quoted, accepted, scheduled, in progress, complete — so you always know exactly where each one is up to, without having to hold it in your head.

That's the model behind TileIQ's job management: a single pipeline for every job, and a dashboard that shows what needs attention today — new enquiries, quotes going quiet, and this week's schedule — the moment you open the app.

Chase-up reminders instead of relying on memory

The jobs that get lost usually aren't lost because of a bad decision — they're lost because nothing flagged that a quote had gone quiet, or that an invoice was still unpaid two weeks later. Automatic chase-up reminders turn "I should really follow that up" into something the system surfaces for you.

Every customer, every job, in one record

Customer details, job history and messages should stay attached to the customer, not scattered across separate text threads and call logs. Save a customer's details once, and reuse them instantly for repeat work — no re-entering an address or a phone number you've already typed in twice before.

You don't need more hours in the day. You need fewer places for a job to quietly fall through.

It works whether you're on your own or running a small team

A simple pipeline works just as well for a sole trader running their own book of jobs as it does for a small tiling team, where everyone needs to be working from the same information rather than whoever remembered to update the group chat.

And because enquiries can arrive by phone, web form, or the AI Receptionist answering a missed call, every new lead lands in the same pipeline automatically — nothing has to be typed in twice, and nothing gets missed because it arrived a different way.

Start with the pipeline, the rest follows

You don't need five different tools to run a tiling business properly — you need one system that keeps enquiries, quotes, jobs and invoices in the same place, with a dashboard that tells you what needs doing today. That's what TileIQ is.
