How much should I charge for tanking a bathroom?

Updated 20 October 2026 · 2 min read · By the TileIQ team

Quick answer

UK trade pricing guides put tanking at about £25–£50 per m², or £150–£400 for a shower area (trade2base, June 2026).

For a full wet room, MyBuilder lists £300–£1,200 for installing the waterproofing, and £500–£1,200 for the tanking kit and membranes (August 2026). Always price tanking as its own line on the quote.

Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water gets through grout joints over time, so every shower, wet room and bath surround needs a waterproof layer behind the tiles. Tanking is quick to skip and very expensive to put right later, so it deserves its own line on the quote.

For context, a complete wet room costs around £5,000–£13,000 on average (Checkatrade, June 2026), and takes 4–7 days. Waterproofing is a small part of that cost, but it's the part that causes the most damage if it fails.

What a tanking price should cover

  • Priming the substrate, if the system needs it.
  • Tape and corner pieces at every internal and external corner, and at the joint between wall and floor.
  • Collars around pipes and the valve, and sealing around the drain or tray.
  • The coats the manufacturer specifies. Liquid systems usually need at least two.
  • Drying time before tiling. This is often what pushes a shower into a second day.

How much to tank: trade2base's wet room guide suggests tanking the whole floor of a wet room and at least 300 mm up every wall, more near the shower head. In a shower enclosure, tank the full tiled area inside the enclosure.

Per m² or per area?

Per m² works for large areas like wet room floors and full walls. For a single shower, a fixed price per area is simpler for the customer and protects you on small jobs. Most of the time goes on corners and pipe collars, not the flat area. The £150–£400 shower-area range above reflects that.

Prices come from published UK guides (see Sources) and vary by region and system. Follow the manufacturer's instructions for the tanking system you use. Coverage, coats and drying times differ between products.
