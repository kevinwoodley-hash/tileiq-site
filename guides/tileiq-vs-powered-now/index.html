TileIQ vs Powered Now: which app suits a tiler?

Updated 23 October 2026 · 2 min read · By the TileIQ team

Quick answer

Powered Now is a UK job app for all trades, with quotes, invoices, a diary, Making Tax Digital and, on higher plans, certificates and team tools. It costs from £28 a month plus VAT.

TileIQ is built only for tilers: it measures rooms, works out tiles, adhesive, grout and tanking, and takes the job through to invoicing and review requests. Its founding offer is £9.99 a month.

Where Powered Now is a good fit

Powered Now is a well-established UK trade app. It runs on almost any device, works offline, and has Making Tax Digital built in on every plan. Higher plans add certificates (useful if you also do electrical or gas work), staff tracking, team chat, purchase orders, a customer portal and automatic quote and invoice chasing. If you run a team across several trades, it covers a lot.

Where TileIQ is a good fit

Powered Now is built for every trade, so the tiling maths is still up to you. TileIQ does it for you:

  • Measure and price together: each wall and floor, or straight from a Leica Disto.
  • Materials worked out: tiles, boxes, adhesive, grout, tanking and prep, with wastage for the layout.
  • Your labour rates on every quote: per m² for walls and floors.
  • After the job: automatic Google review requests and social posts from your job photos.
  • Lower price for a sole trader: £9.99 a month on the founding offer.

Price

Powered Now's Business plan is £28 a month plus VAT for one user. Professional is £32 and Premium £40 per user per month, plus VAT. TileIQ's founding offer is £9.99 a month or £79.99 a year, kept while you stay subscribed.

Which should you choose?

  • You do several trades, need certificates, or run a team: Powered Now.
  • You want Making Tax Digital inside the job app itself: Powered Now.
  • You're a tiler and want measuring, materials and quoting done for you: TileIQ.
  • You measure with a Leica Disto: TileIQ.
