HOW THE NUMBERS WORK

How much adhesive do I need for 35m²?

As a planning estimate: at a typical coverage rate of around 4kg per m² (roughly what a 6mm notched trowel uses for a single fix), 35m² needs about 140kg of adhesive — seven 20kg bags. Grout at a medium joint width (around 0.6kg per m²) would need about 21kg, or five 5kg bags. Always confirm against your specific product's coverage chart, since it changes with trowel notch size, tile size and how flat the substrate is.

Tile wastage

A wastage allowance of around 10% is a common starting point for straight lay patterns, and higher for diagonal, herringbone or complex cuts.

Adhesive coverage

Coverage depends on trowel notch size and whether it's a single or solid bed fix — check the bag for the exact figure.

Grout coverage

Wider joints and larger tiles both increase how much grout a job needs per m².
