Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

TileIQ Pro is a professional job management app for UK tiling contractors. Your data belongs to you — we never sell it or use it for advertising.

What We Collect

Your email address, company details, jobs, customer names and contact details, and device push notification token.

Analytics & Cookies

We only use analytics if you agree. When you first visit tile-iq.com we ask whether you accept analytics cookies, and the TileIQ Pro app asks the first time you open it. Until you accept, Google Analytics is not loaded, no analytics cookies are set and no usage data is sent. If you reject, it stays off.

If you accept, we use Google Analytics 4 (and, in the mobile app, Google's Firebase Analytics) to collect anonymous usage statistics: which pages and screens are viewed, which features are used (for example that a room estimate was completed or a quote was sent), whether the demo or the full app was used, the type of device, and a randomly generated identifier that Google sets (the _ga cookies on the website, an app-instance ID in the app). We never send your name, email address, phone number, address, your customers' details, or the contents or prices of your quotes and invoices to Google Analytics. We do not use advertising cookies or sell this data.

Anonymous visit counts on tile-iq.com. Separately from Google Analytics, our website counts where its visitors come from, so we know which of our posts, ads and listings are worth doing. When you arrive on the site from somewhere else, and when you tap an App Store or Google Play link, your browser tells our server only: the source of the visit (for example "facebook", "tiktok", "google search" or the website that linked to us, worked out from the link you followed or the page you came from), the campaign name if our link carried one, the page you landed on, and whether you're on a phone or a computer. Nothing is stored on your device, no cookies are used, and we don't record your IP address, browser details or anything else that could identify you, so these figures are only ever counts. They are kept for 13 months. Google Play links on our site also carry the same source to Google Play, so we can see which channel led to an app install.

PostHog on tile-iq.com. If you accept analytics on our website, we also use PostHog (EU-hosted, sent through our own domain) to see how the site is used: the pages you visit, where your visit came from (for example a Facebook post or a Google search), what you click, and screen recordings of the pages, with anything you type into a form hidden before it leaves your browser. PostHog keeps a random identifier in your browser's storage so the pages of one visit, and a later visit, can be linked; it isn't linked to your name, email or TileIQ account. If you reject, or change your mind later in "Cookie settings", PostHog isn't loaded and its identifier is removed.

Trying the demo on our website. If you accept analytics on tile-iq.com and then try the demo, we also keep an anonymous record of the steps you take in it (which screens you open, which features you try, whether you tap an App Store or Google Play link, and where you stop), together with where your visit came from, which of our pages sent you to the demo and whether you're on a phone or a computer. Each visit gets a new random number when the page loads; it isn't linked to you, an account, your IP address or your device. With the same permission, PostHog (EU-hosted) records how the demo's screens are used, with every piece of text and everything you type hidden before it leaves your browser. These records are kept for 90 days. If you don't accept, none of this happens.

App usage linked to your account. If you allow usage statistics in the TileIQ Pro app, the app also sends TileIQ (not Google) a record of the steps you take in it, so we can see where people get stuck and make the app easier to use: which screens you open, which features you use (for example adding a room or sending a quote), when you leave the app and come back, and which screen or section you were on when you left. This record is linked to your TileIQ account. It never includes your customers' details, job addresses, or the contents or prices of your quotes and invoices. It is kept for 90 days and then deleted, is only seen by TileIQ, and stops as soon as you turn usage statistics off in Settings → Analytics.

Screen recordings (PostHog). With the same permission, the app uses PostHog, a product analytics service, hosted in the EU, to record how the app's screens are used: what you tap, where you scroll and which screens you move between, so we can see where the app is confusing. In these recordings every piece of text, everything you type, and all photos and images are hidden before anything leaves your device, so they never contain your customers' details, job addresses, photos, or the contents or prices of your quotes and invoices. Recordings and events are linked to your TileIQ account (your account ID and email) so we can understand an individual journey, are only seen by TileIQ, and stop as soon as you turn usage statistics off in Settings → Analytics.

With the same permission, PostHog also receives technical information that helps us fix problems: errors that happen in the app, how quickly screens load, and the timing of the app's requests to our servers (never what those requests contain), plus a few account details for comparing groups of users: your plan and trial status, region, app version, sign-up date and how many jobs, quotes and invoices you have created (counts only, never their contents). We may occasionally show a short in-app question asking about your experience; answering is optional.

If you subscribe, our billing provider (RevenueCat) tells us when a payment, trial conversion or cancellation is confirmed. If you have allowed analytics, we record that event against the same anonymous app identifier; if you haven't, we don't.

You can change your choice at any time: on the website, use the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of any page; in the app, go to Settings → Analytics. Turning it off stops collection and removes the analytics cookies from your browser. Cookies or storage that are essential for signing in and saving your work are always used and do not need consent.

Tiler Portfolio Websites

TileIQ users can publish a portfolio of their work at tile-iq.com/p/<their business name>. Each tiler decides what appears there: only projects they publish, the photos they choose to show (with location data removed), and the business details they choose to share. Customer names, addresses, prices and job notes are never shown. We don't use analytics or cookies on these pages. If you send a quote request through a tiler's page, your details go to that tiler so they can reply, and are handled as described above.

How It's Stored

All data is stored in Supabase (EU-hosted) with row-level security — only you can access your own data. All connections are encrypted over HTTPS.

Your Rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data at any time. Contact us at info@tile-iq.com to exercise these rights.

Third-Party Services

Supabase (database), Resend (email), Cloudflare (hosting), Firebase (push notifications), RevenueCat (subscriptions), Google Analytics / Firebase Analytics (usage statistics, only if you accept — see Analytics & Cookies above), PostHog (EU-hosted product analytics and screen recordings with all text, typing and images hidden, only if you accept). Accounting integrations (FreeAgent, QuickBooks, Xero) are optional and only activated by you.

Google Calendar (optional): if you choose to connect your Google Calendar, TileIQ Pro creates and updates calendar events for the jobs you schedule in the app, and displays your other existing Google Calendar events read-only alongside your jobs so you can see your full schedule in one place while planning work. We do not modify, delete or share any event we did not create ourselves. Calendar event data is exchanged directly between your device and Google's own servers — it is never sent to, or stored on, our own backend. You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time from within the app, which revokes our access.

Google User Data & AI Features

TileIQ Pro uses third-party AI services (Anthropic's Claude models, and ElevenLabs for voice synthesis) for features such as the AI phone receptionist and generated call greetings. These AI features are entirely separate from Google Calendar: no data obtained through Google APIs (including calendar events, titles, times or attendees) is ever transferred to, processed by, or used to train any AI/ML model, by us or by any third party.

We do not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to any third party for advertising, or use it to train generalised AI/ML models. The use of any data received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. On account deletion, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days.
