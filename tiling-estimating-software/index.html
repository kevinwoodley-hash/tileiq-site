01
WHY MANUAL ESTIMATING GOES WRONG

Small errors compound fast.

Round a wastage percentage down, forget a coverage rate, misjudge a labour multiplier — and a tight-margin job becomes an unprofitable one, or a winnable job gets overpriced.

Under-ordering

Running short mid-job means a wasted trip, a delay, and a customer watching the schedule slip.

Over-ordering

Padding every estimate "to be safe" quietly eats into your margin, job after job.

Inconsistent pricing

Estimating by feel means similar jobs get priced differently — and it's hard to say why.

02
MEASURE & CALCULATE

The same calculation, every time.

Add each room as a full room, floor only, walls only or a shower, enter the measurements, and TileIQ works out the tiles and materials for every wall and floor.

Room measurements

Length, width and wall height for each room, with extra walls and floors where you need them. Connect a compatible Leica Disto by Bluetooth to send measurements straight in, or type them from a tape.

Tile quantities with wastage

The area of each wall and floor plus your wastage allowance, so you order enough without padding. Try the same maths in the free tile calculator.

Adhesive & grout by coverage

Worked out from standard coverage rates for the tile and bed, not a rough guess per bag. See the figures in how much tile adhesive you need.

Tile type & prep

Ceramic, porcelain, natural stone, mosaic, herringbone and patterns each carry their own labour, plus wall prep, tanking, floor prep and underfloor heating.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can I try TileIQ without signing up?

Yes. Open the real app in your browser and use it straight away: you can send unlimited quotes for your first 2 weeks with no account or card. After that there's a 30-day free trial. If you'd rather just look around first, the demo has sample jobs and saves nothing.

How does TileIQ estimate wastage?

Tile quantities are calculated from your room measurements with a wastage allowance factored in, so you order enough without excessive over-ordering.

Does it account for different tile sizes and layouts?

Yes — tile type and labour multipliers mean the estimate reflects the actual job, not a flat rate applied to every quote.

Can I estimate a job without a laser measure?

Yes, you can measure with a tape measure and enter dimensions manually, or connect a compatible Leica Disto for faster, hands-free measuring.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. TileIQ is on the App Store and Google Play, and the same app runs in your browser. It works offline and syncs when you're back online.
