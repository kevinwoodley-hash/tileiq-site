Classroom exercise

The TileIQ Bathroom Challenge

Give students a realistic mock customer project and ask them to complete both the practical thinking and the business workflow around it.

Example brief: A customer wants an existing bathroom retiled. Measure the room, determine the materials required, build the price and produce a professional quotation. Then take the mock job through acceptance, scheduling and invoicing in TileIQ.

Designed from experience in the trade.

TileIQ has not been built as generic business software and then adapted for tilers. It was developed by a working tiler around the estimating, quoting and administration problems that happen on real tiling jobs.

That makes the Trade Starter Programme a natural complement to practical training: students learn the craft from their training provider and learn a professional commercial workflow with software built around their trade.
