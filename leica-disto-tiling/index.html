FAQ

Common questions.

Which Leica Disto models does TileIQ work with?

TileIQ connects to a compatible Leica Disto by Bluetooth. If you're not sure whether your specific model supports Bluetooth measurement transfer, check your device's documentation, or get in touch and we'll help you confirm.

Do I need a Leica Disto to use TileIQ?

No — you can measure with a tape measure and enter dimensions manually. The Leica Disto integration is there to speed things up if you already use one.

Does the connection need an internet connection?

No, pairing and measuring works over Bluetooth. TileIQ syncs your job data to the cloud once you're back online.
