TileIQ

Made for: tilers only. Built by a working tiler in Oxfordshire.

  • Measure each wall and floor, or send measurements straight from a Leica Disto by Bluetooth.
  • Works out tiles, boxes, adhesive, grout, tanking and prep, with wastage for the layout, using your own labour rates.
  • Quick quotes, or full itemised quotes with your logo, sent by email or WhatsApp.
  • Job pipeline, calendar and customer records, then invoices to Xero, FreeAgent or QuickBooks.
  • Google review requests, social posts from job photos and a free portfolio website.
  • Works offline. iPhone, Android or any web browser.

Price: founding offer £9.99 a month or £79.99 a year, kept while you stay subscribed. 2 weeks of unlimited free quotes with no account, then a 30-day free trial.

Tradify

Made for: trade businesses of all kinds, from sole traders to larger teams.

  • Jobs, scheduling, quotes, invoices and payments.
  • Timesheets, job costing and recurring jobs on higher plans.
  • Links to Xero, Sage and QuickBooks Online.

Price: from about £34 per user per month, with a 14-day free trial. Text messages cost extra. See TileIQ vs Tradify.

Traddie

Made for: UK tradespeople across many trades.

  • Quotes customers can accept online, invoices with card payment links, and automatic chasing of late payments.
  • TradeStack HQ's review notes square-metre quoting, with tanking priced as a separate line.
  • Certificates, VAT and CIS tracking, and links to Xero, QuickBooks, FreeAgent and Sage.
  • Works offline. iPhone and Android apps.

Price: from £9.99 a month, with a 14-day free trial.

Powered Now

Made for: UK trades of all kinds.

  • Quotes, invoices, diary and job management, with Making Tax Digital built in.
  • Certificates, team tracking and purchase orders on higher plans.
  • Works offline. iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and PC.

Price: from £28 a month plus VAT for one user, with a 14-day free trial and no card needed. See TileIQ vs Powered Now.

Mucka

Made for: UK trades of all kinds.

  • Voice-first: describe the job out loud and its AI assistant writes the quote or invoice.
  • Job management and scheduling, with Xero and QuickBooks links and Making Tax Digital support.
  • iPhone, Android and web.

Price: free version with basic features. Paid plans from £29 a month for solo tradespeople and £40 for teams. See TileIQ vs Mucka.

Tilers Mate

Made for: tilers, focused on quoting.

  • Runs in a web browser, with nothing to install.
  • Material quantities for tiles, adhesive, grout, boards and sealer, with multi-room jobs.
  • Quotes and VAT invoices with your logo, sent by email or WhatsApp, plus supplier order emails.

Price: £9.99 a month founder offer (normally £14.99), or £99 a year. See TileIQ vs Tilers Mate.

Topps Tiles Trade

Made for: buying tiles and materials, not running jobs. It works alongside a quoting app rather than instead of one.

  • Real-time stock checks across Topps Tiles' 280+ stores.
  • Trade pricing and app-only deals, with a rewards scheme.
  • Order tracking, digital receipts, a tile calculator and pay-later trade credit.

How to choose

  • Do you want the tiling maths done for you? General trade apps leave tiles, adhesive, grout and tanking quantities to you. TileIQ and Tilers Mate work them out.
  • Do you work alone or with a team? Per-user pricing adds up quickly if you take someone on.
  • Do you need it to work without signal? Check before you rely on it on site.
  • Which accounting software do you use? Check the app links to it.
  • Try before you pay. Most offer a free trial, so price one real job in each and see which is quickest.
