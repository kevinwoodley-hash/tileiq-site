Where Mucka is a good fit

Mucka's big idea is voice. You describe the job out loud and its AI assistant turns that into a quote or invoice, which is handy if you hate typing on a phone. It works for any trade, links to Xero and QuickBooks, supports Making Tax Digital, and has a free version to start on. If you do several kinds of work, not just tiling, a general trade app may suit you better.

Where TileIQ is a good fit

A tiling quote lives or dies on the measurements and materials. TileIQ is built around that:

  • Measure once: enter each wall and floor, or send measurements straight from a Leica Disto.
  • Materials worked out for you: tiles, boxes, adhesive, grout, tanking and prep, with wastage for the layout.
  • Your rates, every time: labour per m² for walls and floors, so quotes are consistent.
  • The whole job: enquiries, calendar, invoices to Xero, FreeAgent or QuickBooks, then review requests and a portfolio website.
  • On site without signal: it works offline and syncs later.

Price

Mucka has a free version, and its paid plans start at £29 a month for a solo tradesperson and £40 for teams. TileIQ's founding offer is £9.99 a month or £79.99 a year, kept while you stay subscribed, with your first quote free and a 30-day trial. Check both for current prices.

Which should you choose?

  • You do several trades, or you'd rather talk than type: Mucka.
  • You're a tiler and want the measuring and materials done for you: TileIQ.
  • You measure with a Leica Disto: TileIQ.

Both let you try for free, so the best test is to quote the same real job in each.
