A shower is a small area but slow to tile. Every wall has cuts around the tray, valve, outlets and corners, and it all has to be waterproof behind the tiles. That's why showers are usually priced as a job rather than straight per m².

For comparison, a complete walk-in shower installation, including the tray, screen and plumbing, typically costs £2,100–£2,500 (Checkatrade, March 2026). The tiling is one part of that.

What moves the price up or down

  • Wall area: a 900 mm tray with two tiled walls to 2 m high is about 4 m². A 1,700 mm walk-in with three full-height walls can be 10 m² or more.
  • Tile: large format, natural stone and mosaic all take longer. Published rates for those sit at the top of the per m² range.
  • Tanking and boarding: needed in every shower. Price them as separate lines (see tanking prices).
  • Niches, mitred corners and trims: each one adds time.
  • Strip-out: removing old tiles and repairing the wall behind them.
