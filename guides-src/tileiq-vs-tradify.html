Where Tradify is a good fit

Tradify is a mature, general job management system. If you run a team, its scheduling, timesheets, job costing, purchase orders and service reminders are built for that, and it links to Xero, Sage and QuickBooks Online. If tiling is only part of what your business does, a general app like Tradify covers every job type in one place.

Where TileIQ is a good fit

General job apps leave the tiling maths to you. TileIQ does it:

  • Quick quote: when you just need a price fast, without setting up a full job.
  • Measure and price in one go: walls and floors, room by room, or straight from a Leica Disto.
  • Materials worked out: tiles, boxes, adhesive, grout, tanking and prep, with wastage for the layout.
  • Quote to invoice: the same job flows through, sent to Xero, FreeAgent or QuickBooks.
  • After the job: review requests, social posts from your photos and a free portfolio website.
  • Priced for a sole trader: one low monthly price rather than per user.

Price

Tradify is priced per user, from about £34 per user per month, with text messages charged extra. TileIQ's founding offer is £9.99 a month or £79.99 a year, kept while you stay subscribed. For a one-person tiling business that's a big difference. For a team that needs timesheets and job costing, Tradify's extra features may be worth it.

Which should you choose?

  • You run a multi-trade team and need timesheets, job costing and purchase orders: Tradify.
  • You use Sage: Tradify links to it.
  • You're a tiler and want measuring, materials and quoting done for you: TileIQ.
  • You're a sole trader watching costs: TileIQ.
