Where Tilers Mate is a good fit

Tilers Mate is focused on one job: getting an accurate quote out quickly. It runs in a web browser, so there's nothing to install, and it handles multi-room jobs, automatic wastage on large-format tiles, and quotes and VAT invoices with your logo. It can also email a pro-forma order straight to your supplier, which TileIQ doesn't do yet.

If you already run your diary and customers somewhere else and just want faster quotes, it's a straightforward choice.

Where TileIQ is a good fit

TileIQ does a quick quote when you just need a price fast, and can also run the whole job:

  • Measuring: enter room sizes, or connect a Leica Disto by Bluetooth and send measurements straight in.
  • Pricing: tiles, adhesive, grout, tanking and prep for every wall and floor, using your own labour rates.
  • Running the job: a job pipeline, calendar and customer records, so enquiries don't get lost.
  • Getting paid: invoices from the accepted quote, with Xero, FreeAgent and QuickBooks.
  • After the job: automatic Google review requests, social posts from your job photos, and a free portfolio website.
  • No signal needed: it works offline on site and syncs when you're back online.

Price

At the time of writing the two cost about the same per month. TileIQ's founding offer is £9.99 a month or £79.99 a year, and you keep that price while you stay subscribed. Tilers Mate lists a £9.99 a month founder offer, normally £14.99, or £99 a year. Founder offers on both apps may end, so check current prices.

Which should you choose?

  • You want quick quotes, with room to grow: TileIQ does quick quotes too, plus the rest of the job when you need it.
  • You want enquiries, quotes, scheduling, invoices and reviews in one place: TileIQ.
  • You measure with a Leica Disto, or often work without signal: TileIQ.

Both let you try before you pay, so the quickest way to decide is to price one real job in each.
