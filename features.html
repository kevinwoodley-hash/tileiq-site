How can tilers calculate materials and labour?

Measure each room by hand or with a Leica Disto, and TileIQ works out the tiles, adhesive, grout and wastage for every wall and floor. QUESTIONS

Common questions about TileIQ.

Can I really send a quote without signing up?

Yes. Download TileIQ from the App Store or Google Play and you can use the app and send your first real quote to a customer without signing up. After that you can use TileIQ free for 30 days, without subscribing.

Does TileIQ work without an internet connection?

Yes. You can measure, quote and manage jobs with no signal, and everything syncs automatically once you are back online.

Do I need a Leica Disto to use TileIQ?

No. A Leica Disto connected by Bluetooth sends measurements straight into the app, but you can type measurements in by hand just as well. See the Leica Disto tiling app.

Does TileIQ work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. TileIQ is on the App Store and Google Play, and your jobs sync across your devices.

Does TileIQ connect to Xero, QuickBooks or FreeAgent?

Yes. You can send invoices from TileIQ into Xero, QuickBooks or FreeAgent, so you do not have to re-enter the customer or job details. More on the invoice app for tilers.

Do I need my own website for the portfolio?

No. Your portfolio is hosted for you with its own link that you can share anywhere. If you already own a domain name, you can show the portfolio on it instead. See how to create a tiling portfolio without a website.
