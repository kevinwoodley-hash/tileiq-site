01
ALREADY GOT THE APP?

You can join from inside TileIQ.

  1. Open TileIQ and sign in.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Under Help Improve TileIQ, switch on Join the testers.

Make sure TileIQ is up to date in the App Store or Google Play first.

02
WHAT'S INVOLVED

No forms. No meetings. Just use it.

  • Use TileIQ as normalQuote, book and invoice your own jobs the way you always would.
  • The odd quick questionStraight after you use a feature, one question pops up. One tap to answer, plus a comment if you want to say more.
  • Never more than two a weekAnd you can close any question without answering.
  • Your answers, not your customers'Answers are linked to your account so Kevin can follow up. Never your customers, quotes or prices.
  • Leave whenever you likeSwitch it off in Settings → Help Improve TileIQ. Nothing else changes.
03
FAQ

Common questions.

Does it cost anything?

Being a tester is free. TileIQ itself has a 30-day free trial with no card needed.

Who's it for?

Tilers in the UK who quote and run their own jobs. You don't need to be good with tech, that's the point.

Do I need a TileIQ account?

Yes, a free one, so your answers stay with you. You can create it in the app or on the web.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. TileIQ Pro is on the App Store and Google Play, and you can also join on the web.

Can I stop?

Any time: Settings → Help Improve TileIQ → switch off Join the testers.
